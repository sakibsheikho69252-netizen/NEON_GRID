PRIVACY POLICY

NEON GRID · LAST UPDATED: 2025

Short Version

NEON GRID does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. The game runs entirely in your browser and works fully offline.

What We Store

We use your browser's built-in localStorage to save your game progress locally on your own device. This includes:

  • Unlocked levels and star ratings
  • Best times and best move counts
  • Achievement progress
  • Daily Puzzle results
  • Your settings (sound, vibration, reduced effects)

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to any server.

What We Do Not Do

  • No account or login required
  • No cookies for tracking
  • No analytics or telemetry
  • No advertising networks
  • No third-party scripts, fonts, or CDNs
  • No personal information collected

Sound & Vibration

Sound is generated in real time using the Web Audio API. Vibration (if enabled) uses the standard browser Vibration API. Both can be turned off at any time in Settings.

Sharing

When you tap "Share Result", your device's native share dialog opens. The text you share contains only your game score — no personal data.

Children

The game is safe for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting Your Data

Open Settings → "Reset All Progress" to erase all locally stored data. Clearing your browser data will also remove it.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on the GitHub repository.
