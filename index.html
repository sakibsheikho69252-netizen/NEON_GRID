How To Play

Basics

Tap any tile to rotate it 90° clockwise. Rotate tiles until every receiver is powered by the energy source.

Power flows through tiles only when both touching edges have an open circuit line. A gap breaks the circuit.

Goal

Every receiver (green ring) must be lit. When the whole circuit is complete, the level is solved.

Scoring

Earn up to 3 stars. Use fewer moves, finish faster, and avoid hints for a perfect score.

Special Tiles

  • Source — the pink pulsing node. Energy starts here.
  • Receiver — green ring. Must be powered.
  • Locked — has a padlock, cannot be rotated.
  • One-Way — energy only flows in the arrow direction.
  • Portal — two linked nodes. Power jumps between them.
  • Empty cell — no tile. Nothing can pass through.

Keyboard (desktop)

  • Click a tile to rotate it
  • Z or U — Undo
  • R — Reset level
  • H — Hint
  • Esc — Pause
